How to Get Real-Time PhD & Postdoc Job Alerts on Telegram (Free)

Published by drLacheheb • 5 min read

The traditional method of searching for academic positions—manually browsing 5 to 10 portals or subscribing to cluttered email newsletters that send weekly digests—often results in missed application deadlines.

By routing notifications to Telegram, you receive instant, actionable mobile push notifications the moment a position matching your research profile is published.

Step 1: Start the Academix Bot

Open Telegram and initiate a conversation with @academix_bot. Press /start to initialize your candidate session.

Step 2: Upload Your Academic CV (PDF)

Upload your current CV as a PDF file directly in the chat.

Behind the scenes, the Academix pipeline executes an autonomous ingestion workflow:

  • PDF Ingestion & Text Extraction: Extracts structural headings, degrees, publications, and skills.
  • Multilingual Translation (NLLB-200): Translates foreign-language qualifications or institution titles into standardized English.
  • Structured Metadata Refinement: Classifies your highest degree tier (Master, PhD) and extracts specific research disciplines.
  • Vector Embedding Generation: Generates 768-dimensional dense vector embeddings using nomic-embed-text-v1.5.

Step 3: Receive Push Alerts with Match Reasoning

Whenever discovery crawlers ingest newly published positions from EURAXESS, AcademicTransfer, ABG, Nature Careers, ResearchGate, or EuroScienceJobs, the matching engine computes your compatibility score in real time.

If a position meets your compatibility threshold (e.g., >75%), the bot sends a push alert containing:

  • Job Title & Host Institution
  • Application Deadline
  • Match Score Percentage
  • Personalized Match Summary (Explaining why your specific skills and research focus match the lab's requirements)
  • Direct One-Click Application Link
