The 6 Best Academic Job Boards for PhDs & Postdocs in 2026 (Compared)

Published by drLacheheb • 7 min read

Securing a fully funded doctoral fellowship, postdoctoral researcher position, or tenure-track professorship requires navigating a heavily fragmented landscape. Unlike generic corporate recruitment where LinkedIn and Indeed dominate, the international academic job market relies on specialized regional, governmental, and publisher-backed portals.

Below is an authoritative comparison of the top 6 academic job platforms in 2026, evaluating their geographic scope, strengths, and unique listings.

1. EURAXESS: The Gold Standard for European Mobility

Supported directly by the European Commission, EURAXESS is mandatory viewing for anyone seeking academic research positions in Europe. It is the primary publishing destination for Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Networks and Postdoctoral Fellowships, as well as European Research Council (ERC) funded lab hires.

Best for: Candidates looking for international research mobility and guaranteed EU standard compensation packages.

2. AcademicTransfer: The Dutch Academic Powerhouse

In the Netherlands, PhD candidates are hired as salaried university employees under the Dutch Collective Labour Agreement (CAO-NU), receiving full employment benefits, pensions, and vacation allowances. AcademicTransfer is the exclusive official portal co-owned by all Dutch research universities, university medical centers (UMCs), and research institutes (such as NWO and TNO).

Best for: Anyone targeting funded PhD or postdoc appointments in the Netherlands.

3. Nature Careers: Top-Tier Global STEM Positions

Backed by the prestigious Springer Nature publishing group, Nature Careers features premier postdoctoral fellowships, department chair roles, and senior scientific positions in high-impact laboratories across the United States, United Kingdom, Europe, and Asia.

Best for: Established researchers and postdocs in biology, chemistry, physics, and medical sciences.

4. ResearchGate: Network-Powered Recruitment

ResearchGate leverages its network of over 25 million scientists. When principal investigators post job vacancies, candidate applications are contextualized by their real-world publication record, RG Score, and citation index.

Best for: Candidates with an active preprint and publication portfolio seeking direct connections with lab heads.

5. ABG (Association Bernard Gregory): The French Doctoral Hub

For research positions in France, ABG is the premier platform. It specializes in French Ministry doctoral contracts, CNRS laboratory postings, and CIFRE industrial doctorates (public-private research partnerships between companies and universities).

Best for: Candidates targeting French PhDs, CIFRE funding, and European industrial R&D positions.

6. EuroScienceJobs: Niche European Science & Policy

EuroScienceJobs focuses strictly on post-graduate scientific employment across Europe, spanning university research, international scientific NGOs, and commercial pharma/biotech labs.

Best for: Applied research scientists and scientific policy specialists.
