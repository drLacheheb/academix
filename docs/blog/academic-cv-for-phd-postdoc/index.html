How to Write an Academic CV for PhD & Postdoc Applications (AI & ATS Friendly)

Published by drLacheheb • 7 min read

Writing an Academic Curriculum Vitae (CV) requires a completely different approach from a corporate resume. While industry resumes prioritize brevity (1–2 pages) and business KPIs, an academic CV emphasizes research trajectory, scientific publications, independent funding, and methodological expertise.

Furthermore, as major universities and platforms adopt automated parsing systems and AI matching tools, ensuring your CV is formatted for machine readability is critical.

1. The Standard Academic CV Structure

To ensure clarity for both principal investigators (PIs) and AI parsers, use standard, unambiguous section headers:

  1. Header & Contact Information: Full name, current institutional affiliation, professional email, ORCID ID, and links to Google Scholar / ResearchGate.
  2. Research Interests / Summary: A 3–5 line executive summary defining your primary research domains and methodological focus.
  3. Education: Reverse-chronological list of degrees (PhD, Master's, Bachelor's) including thesis titles, advisor names, and graduation honors.
  4. Academic & Research Appointments: Research assistantships, postdoc positions, or visiting scholar appointments.
  5. Publications: Structured and categorized by type (Peer-Reviewed Journal Articles, Conference Proceedings, Preprints/Under Review).
  6. Grants, Fellowships & Awards: Competitive funding secured, travel grants, and academic prizes.
  7. Technical & Methodological Skills: Laboratory techniques, statistical software, computational languages, and instruments.
  8. Teaching & Mentorship: Courses taught, guest lectures, and undergraduate researchers supervised.

2. Publication Formatting Best Practices

  • Bold Your Name in the author list (e.g., “Smith, J., Doe, A., & Taylor, R.”) to make your authorship contribution instantly visible.
  • Include DOIs or hyperlinked publication links for every article.
  • Separate In-Press / Submitted Papers from finalized peer-reviewed publications.

3. ATS & AI Machine-Readability Rules

Modern recruitment pipelines (including Academix's neural parser) convert uploaded PDFs into structured semantic vectors. To ensure your credentials are parsed with 100% accuracy:

  • Use a Clean Single-Column Layout: Avoid multi-column magazine layouts or sidebars, which cause parsers to scramble adjacent lines.
  • Avoid Text Hidden in Images or Graphics: Store all contact information, skills, and dates as selectable, searchable text.
  • Use Standard Dates (YYYY or MM/YYYY): Clear date formatting allows algorithms to verify your degree level timelines.
