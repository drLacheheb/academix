How Academix Crawls & Ingests 20,000+ Listings Across 6 Academic Portals

Published by drLacheheb • 7 min read

Academic recruitment is fragmented across dozens of regional and international portals. Candidates seeking doctoral fellowships, postdoctoral associate roles, and tenure-track professorships often struggle to aggregate opportunities across borders.

Academix solves this by deploying a distributed suite of 12 specialized crawler agents (6 discovery + 6 sourcing) that harvest, parse, and normalize vacancies into clean, structured Markdown ready for NLP translation and semantic matching.

1. The 6 Supported Academic Portals

Academix targets the most influential academic job boards in Europe and worldwide:

  • EURAXESS: The European Commission's flagship researcher mobility portal with over 10,000 European PhD, Postdoc, and Senior Researcher listings.
  • AcademicTransfer: The central recruitment platform for all 14 Dutch research universities, UMCs, and NWO research institutes.
  • ABG (Association Bernard Gregory): The premier French hub for doctorates, CIFRE industrial research grants, and CNRS opportunities.
  • Nature Careers: Global high-impact faculty and postdoctoral openings published in Nature and related Springer Nature journals.
  • ResearchGate: Direct listings from international university departments and industry R&D labs.
  • EuroScienceJobs: European scientific policy, chemistry, biotech, and research administration positions.

2. Full-Scroll Pagination & Sitemap Architecture

Rather than relying on brittle watermark lookbacks that can miss renewed or bumped job postings, Academix utilizes two robust discovery mechanisms:

  • Full-Scroll HTTP Pagination (EURAXESS, ResearchGate, ABG): Discovery agents paginate sequentially from page 1 until no further listings are returned, capturing all active listings.
  • XML Sitemap Traversal (Nature Careers, AcademicTransfer, EuroScienceJobs): Agents fetch official XML sitemaps, parse hierarchical namespace structures, and extract all active vacancy URLs in bulk.

3. Two-Stage Crawling Pattern: Discovery vs Sourcing

Academix isolates URL discovery from detail scraping to achieve maximum concurrency and resilience:

  1. Discovery Workers (*-discovery): Lightweight crawlers that collect basic stubs (title, url, source) and submit them to the FastAPI gateway via POST /jobs.
  2. Sourcing Workers (*-sourcing): Detail fetchers that claim pending jobs via GET /jobs/pending-details, extract tabular metadata, convert HTML DOM to clean Markdown, and update the database via PUT /jobs/details.

4. Summary

By separating discovery and sourcing, and standardizing all listings into rich Markdown with parsed deadlines and institutional metadata, Academix feeds a clean dataset into its local translation and LLM refinement pipeline.
