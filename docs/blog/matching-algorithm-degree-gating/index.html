Semantic Degree Gating & Asymmetric Max-Dominance Vector Matching

Published by drLacheheb • 8 min read

Matching candidates to academic positions is fundamentally different from generic corporate hiring. In academia, strict educational prerequisites (e.g., holding a Master's for a PhD, or holding a Doctorate for a Postdoc) coexist with highly specialized research overlap.

Academix employs a multi-tiered mathematical and semantic funnel to guarantee both strict eligibility and deep research alignment.

1. The 4-Step Scoring Funnel

Every candidate profile is evaluated against active academic positions through 4 successive stages:

  1. Degree Level Compatibility Gate: Verifies that candidate education tier satisfies the minimum required level (PhD ≥ Master ≥ Bachelor ≥ High School). If a candidate holds a Bachelor's degree and the job demands a PhD, the score immediately evaluates to 0.0.
  2. Semantic Degree Field Similarity Gate: Measures cosine similarity between the candidate's degree field and the job's prerequisite degree field using nomic-embed-text-v1.5. A minimum threshold of 0.71 is enforced.
  3. Multi-Vector Embedding Scores: Calculates dot product similarities for Skill Competencies and Research Areas against pre-computed vector embeddings.
  4. Asymmetric Max-Dominance Pooling: Synthesizes individual vector scores into a single composite match score.

2. Asymmetric Max-Dominance Formula

Rather than a naive arithmetic mean that penalizes highly specialized researchers, Academix applies asymmetric dominance pooling:

Composite Score = 0.70 × max(SkillScore, ResearchScore) + 0.30 × min(SkillScore, ResearchScore)

This formula rewards candidates who exhibit extraordinary mastery in either specific methodology or research subject matter, while maintaining a baseline check on secondary qualifications.

3. Automated Match Explanations

When a match exceeds the candidate's threshold (e.g., 0.75), the matching agent invokes the LLM via ExplainMatchUseCase to generate a concise, personalized explanation highlighting why the candidate is qualified for the role.
