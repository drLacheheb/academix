The Complete Guide to EURAXESS Jobs: How to Search & Land EU Research Roles in 2026

Published by drLacheheb • 7 min read

EURAXESS is the European Commission's central initiative supporting researcher mobility and career development across 40+ European countries. With thousands of active vacancies spanning doctoral fellowships, postdoctoral associate roles, and principal investigator grants, EURAXESS is the primary portal for European scientific recruitment.

However, navigating EURAXESS effectively requires understanding its standardized classification system and funding schemes.

1. Understanding the European Researcher Profiles (R1 to R4)

Every job on EURAXESS is categorized according to the official European Framework for Research Careers:

Profile Tier Target Career Stage Typical Roles
R1 — First Stage Researcher Up to the point of PhD Doctoral candidates, PhD fellows, MSCA Doctoral Network researchers
R2 — Recognised Researcher PhD holders / equivalent experience Postdoctoral researchers, research fellows, project scientists
R3 — Established Researcher Independent research leaders Assistant / Associate Professors, Principal Investigators (PIs), Senior Scientists
R4 — Leading Researcher International field leaders Full Professors, Department Chairs, Research Institute Directors

2. Key Funding Mechanisms on EURAXESS

  • Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA): Highly competitive EU fellowships emphasizing cross-border mobility. Positions include generous base living allowances (~€4,010/mo) and mobility allowances (€710/mo).
  • European Research Council (ERC) Grants: Vacancies within ERC Starting, Consolidator, or Advanced grant project teams.
  • Hosting Offers: Academic institutions and research groups interested in hosting researchers who are applying for their own independent fellowships.
